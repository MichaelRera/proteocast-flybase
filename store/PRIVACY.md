# Privacy policy — ProteoCast for FlyBase (browser extension)

*Last updated: 23 October 2026*

ProteoCast for FlyBase is a browser extension that shows ProteoCast missense-variant predictions on FlyBase
(flybase.org) report pages. It does not collect, store or share any personal data.

**What the extension reads.** Only on flybase.org pages: the FlyBase identifier in the page address
(FBpp, FBgn or FBal) and, on allele pages, the associated gene and the amino-acid replacement shown in the
report. It does not read other websites, form contents, or your browsing history.

**What it sends, and to whom.** To display the panel, it requests public data, identified only by a FlyBase ID:
- from FlyBase's public API (api.flybase.org): the protein sequence, protein domains and a gene's isoforms;
- from the ProteoCast server (proteocast.ijm.fr, hosted by the Institut Jacques Monod, Paris): the predictions
  for that protein.

As with any web request, these servers receive your IP address and browser information in their standard
access logs. The extension adds no identifiers, cookies or tracking of its own, and sends nothing else.

**What it stores.** One setting (the prediction-server address), saved in your
browser's extension storage (synchronised by your browser if you use browser sync). Nothing is stored on
any server by the extension.

**No analytics, no advertising, no sale of data.** The extension contains no analytics or advertising code,
and no data is sold or transferred to third parties.
